I work on learning-based agents that perceive, predict, and act under uncertainty. My main interests are **autonomous driving** and **embodied AI**, especially world models, low-data policy adaptation, and evaluation under dynamics or distribution shifts.

## Current Focus

- Vehicle-dynamics-aware world models for autonomous driving
- Few-demonstration adaptation of vision-language-action policies
- Robust evaluation under actuator, environment, and task shifts
- Reproducible research pipelines from baseline validation to failure analysis
